# Wildcard Tavern: Status

Roblox poker-hand roguelike deckbuilder, solo project aiming for the Roblox Home front page: a reason to return daily and about 3 hours of content. Plan: [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). The long per-feature notes that used to live here are in git history (`git log -p claude/STATUS.md`); read the code and its tests for detail.

- **Tests:** `lua tools/run_tests_local.lua` (plain `lua` works, `lua5.3` is not installed here): 616 passing, 0 failing. Includes the sort-order lint, the text-size lint (nothing under 14) and the locals guard.

## What exists
- **Game name:** The Gilded Knave (`Engine/GameName.lua`: NAME, SHORT, empty TAGLINE; repo, place and DataStore keys keep the old working title). The Menu title is `Client/Wordmark.lua`: muted gold Color-only gradient text, dark outline, drawn diamond flourishes, no tagline, no flicker; `Engine/LogoArt.lua` (logo_wordmark, logo_emblem, empty) swaps in the painted wordmark once recorded. Share text, Win Card, seed share and invite prompt use GameName.
- **Run loop:** 8 Nights of Small, Big and Boss rounds, Patrons, Recipes, House Passes, packs, tags, The Counter shop (opens only after a played, cleared round), One More Round (free daily, then paid), 8 stakes, Legend prestige, Quick Run, Boss Rush, challenge runs, Daily, Weekly and Seed runs, Endless with Crowd Rules, start-of-run draft and random events.
- **The Counter:** Peddler's Wagon scene with Marlo the Peddler and his lines (`Client/ShopScene.lua`, `Engine/ShopkeeperLines.lua`), one Haggle per visit on a server-timed needle (`Engine/Haggle.lua`, refused on ranked boards and in the tutorial) and the rare Wandering Stranger with six Dealer's Bargains (`Engine/Strangers.lua`, never Nights 1 to 2 or ranked).
- **The Menu is the tavern:** parallax backdrop, a wooden bar counter with six items (Quest Scroll, Road Ahead, The Tome, Coin Jar, Rulebook, Jukebox) that unroll parchment scrolls of ink-lettered action rows, the Deal Me In sign hung from a ceiling beam, a Notice Board on the left wall with pinned notes (Rank, Next goal, news, today's board), an All actions list (the only view on phones), the painted tavern wall, beams, counter items, scroll rods, seals and a swinging foreground lantern with fire glow, embers, window shaft, candle and steam (16 animated objects, off in calm mode, `Client/MenuScene.lua`), rare tiny motes (`Engine/MenuLayout.lua`, `Client/MenuHotspots.lua`, `MenuBoard.lua`, `MenuParallax.lua`, `MenuScene.lua`). Poker Hands is a ledger board with a detail card (`Engine/HandLedger.lua`). Collection shows progress only; run numbers live in the Tavern Ledger.
- **Bonds and Regulars:** Patron Bonds between Patrons sharing a synergy tag (`Engine/Bonds.lua`, glowing sidebar threads, Collection Bonds tab); Tavern Regulars in the Menu corner for bosses you beat and Patrons you won with (`Engine/Regulars.lua`).
- **Big moments:** Showdown gold banner on a Boss clear (`Client/Showdown.lua`) and a Win Card with a selectable share line after a run win or new best (`Engine/WinCard.lua`, `Client/WinCard.lua`).
- **Bosses:** 43 Boss Rounds with rule tags, Boss Scout and one-time Rule Ban, painted boss portrait with HP bar, taunts, attacks, enrage and defeat beats (`Client/BossFight.lua`, `Engine/BossLines.lua`).
- **Journey:** the Tavern Map (fog, hero, 9 regions, 18 routes, side stops, compass, biome rewards, hover and tap cards on landmarks, stops and discovery tiles) opened from Road Ahead; region joins overlap 30 percent per side (`Engine/MapSeams.lua`, `tools/map_seam_report.lua`); scenes change per Night and round with a walk-in beat.
- **Modals:** Choice, Road, pack and Collection detail popups mount a themed opaque stage (`Client/ModalStage.lua`, `Engine/ModalGate.lua`); while one is open, hover handlers behind it do nothing. Round Select has no Scout bar (the Boss card shows the rule; the Rule Ban button sits top right).
- **Meta:** Card Sets is a Tome tab and a Menu action (Tome scroll, All actions); the equipped set reskins card fronts, panels, buttons, tabs and the HUD through `Client/SetSkin.lua` (slots in `CosmeticSets.skinOf`, index plates and ink from `CosmeticSets.indexStyle`/`indexContrast`, at least 4.5:1 for every set, art map `SetSkinArt.lua` with all 90 Image ids recorded, set colours stand in for any missing slot). Account panels share group tabs (The Tome, The Strongbox, The Coin Jar) with bookmark ribbons for the pages and a Back button (`Client/PanelTabs.lua`, `Engine/PanelGroups.lua`), Daily Rewards ladder (Menu tile and Settings), Legend showpiece, Flair as a Tavern Dressing Room (curtained mirror stage with live preview, a detail card, one equipped slot per category, a cabinet with a sliding drawer per category of round medallions on velvet, locked silhouettes with brass tags and progress rails, gold ring and On ribbon, one clamped tooltip; `Client/FlairDressingRoom.lua`, `Engine/FlairRoomLayout.lua`, art map `FlairUiArt.lua`, all 9 Image ids recorded), Tavern Ledger stats with an expandable run list, Round Select with equal cards and a gold cleared state, Collection with six rarity tiers and detail view, Card Sets and Card Backs showcase with Preview, Badges as a trophy wall whose furniture changes per tab (`Client/BadgeWall.lua`, `Engine/BadgeWallLayout.lua`, art map `BadgeWallArt.lua`, all 11 Image ids recorded), Flair, Tavern Rank, daily quests, login streak, Tavern Season with a premium track, Stats, Your Deck viewer, News and live events, menu ticker, Friends and global leaderboards, Invites, Spectate.
- **Themed HUD:** Play Hand and Discard are code-drawn per Card Set (solid base, two rings in the set accent, Color-only gradient, hover and pressed states, text at 4.5:1), one segmented Rank | Suit sort toggle, the five top-right round buttons (ring, glow, disc tint, motif) and the draw pile (accent corner brackets, themed count plate, three card backs with a per-set tilt) follow the equipped or previewed set (`Engine/HudStyle.lua`, `Client/HudTheme.lua`, `SortToggle.lua`, `DeckWidgetSkin.lua`). Classic Tavern cards wear a code-drawn oak and brass border on the full card edge (`Client/ClassicCardFrame.lua`, at least 6 percent of the width per side). Art hook: `skin.images.hudAccent` for a small button corner accent.
- **Fair monetization:** Fast Forward and Golden Gala passes, One More Round, cosmetic store items and season premium. Nothing changes whether a run is won. Developer Product ids are set in `Engine/GamePasses.lua`; the Season premium and Flair store ids are still placeholders (0).
- **Onboarding and access:** 24 step tutorial with spotlight, first-time hints, How to Play, Poker Hands reference, Settings (volume, animation speed, calm modes, larger text, hold to confirm), phone layout.
- **Art:** 2D painted UI. One pipeline (`tools/patron_art/generate_patron_art.py --category <cat>`, prompts in `prompts.json`) writes generated `*Art.lua` maps in `src/Shared/Engine/`; `ContentArt.getImageId(tab, id)` looks them up and the UI falls back to emoji or flat colour when an id is empty. Gaps are tracked by `python3 tools/art_status.py` and `claude/ART_TODO.md`.
- **Balance:** decent bot win rate by stake 1 to 8 is 29.0 / 26.3 / 23.0 / 20.2 / 16.5 / 12.8 / 7.0 / 6.0 percent (600 runs, no soft locks). Regression bands in `tools/test_sim.lua`.
